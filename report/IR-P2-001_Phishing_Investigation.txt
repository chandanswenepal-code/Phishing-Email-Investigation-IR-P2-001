EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
A phishing email impersonating McAfee™ was received targeting
German-speaking Microsoft Outlook users. The email used a fake
subscription expiry lure with a 24-hour countdown to pressure
victims into clicking malicious URLs. Full header analysis,
SPF/DKIM/DMARC verification, and IOC investigation confirmed
this is a bulk phishing campaign designed to harvest payment
card details and credentials via a fake renewal page hosted
on wheeltiefoot.com. The campaign has 704+ confirmed victim
interactions on URLscan.io.

VERDICT & RECOMMENDATIONS
=============================================================
VERDICT: TRUE POSITIVE — Confirmed Bulk Phishing Campaign

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
  [1] Block sender domain sweeterpasta.de in email gateway
  [2] Block sending IP 4.227.1.199 in email gateway
  [3] Block domains wheeltiefoot.com and hxzf4er.com
      in DNS firewall and web proxy
  [4] Submit IOCs to MISP threat intelligence platform
  [5] Submit wheeltiefoot.com to Google Safe Browsing
  [6] Alert all users who may have received this campaign
  [7] Reset credentials for any user who clicked the link
  [8] Check payment card statements for unauthorized charges
      if any user entered card details

LONG-TERM RECOMMENDATIONS:
  [1] Enforce SPF/DKIM/DMARC on organizational email domain
  [2] Deploy email gateway with URL rewriting/sandboxing
  [3] Implement security awareness training on phishing lures
  [4] Block URL shorteners (t.co, bit.ly) in email gateway
      or force expansion before delivery
  [5] Configure DMARC reporting to detect spoofing attempts
  [6] Raise Microsoft SCL threshold — SCL:5 should quarantine
